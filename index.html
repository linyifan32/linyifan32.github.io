About me

I am a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at Rutgers University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Kangning Wang. My research interests lie at the intersection of economics and computation, with recent work on trade, voting, and online algorithms.

Previously, I earned my bachelor's degree at Shanghai Jiao Tong University as a member of ACM Honors Class, where I had the privilege of beginning my research in theoretical computer science under the guidance of Yuhao Zhang.
